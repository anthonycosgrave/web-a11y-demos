Can TAB to this link outside the toolbar.
  1. You can TAB or SHIFT + TAB into and out of the toolbar.
  2. You can move through the button options with the LEFT and RIGHT arrow keys.
  3. All buttons respond to SPACE and ENTER keys.
  4. The Bold, Italic and Underline buttons can be toggled on and off. They use JS and aria-pressed to manage their state.
  5. Optional additional functionality added:
    1. Press the HOME key to move focus to the first button in the toolbar.
    2. Press the END key to move focus to the last button in the toolbar.
